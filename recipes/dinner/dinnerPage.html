Beef & Broccoli

Ingredients for 1 serving

  • 20g soy sauce
  • 10g oyster sauce
  • 10g rice vinegar
  • 5g Hoisin
  • Chilli flakes
Sauce:
  • 20g soy sauce
  • 10g oyster sauce
  • 10g rice vinegar
  • 5g Hoisin
  • Chilli flakes
Directions:
  1. Start with the washing and marinating first if you choose to do it (go to the chapter “Washing and Marinating Meat to Tenderize”). Otherwise, just cut the meat into strips.
  2. Chop up the broccoli florets and cut them into equal bitesize pieces.
  3. Peel and grate garlic and ginger.
  4. Add all sauce ingredients to a glass and mix until combined.
  5. Prepare a small glass with 50g water, add 2g of cornstarch, and mix, so there are no clumps.
  6. Add broccoli to a pan (24cm/9,5inch) and cover with cold water until submerged. Cook the broccoli florets on a stove on medium-high for 2-3 minutes to get them soft.
  7. Rinse the broccoli and wipe the pan with a paper towel and add in the beef. If you don’t marinate the beef, you need to add a bit of oil to the pan.
  8. Fry the beef for 1 minute on each side so it gets color.
  9. Remove the beef, add oil to the pan and fry the broccoli for 1-2 min, so they have a crunchy exterior with a soft interior
  10. Add back the beef and the grated garlic and ginger. Fry them for 20 seconds.
  11. Add soy sauce and half of the cornstarch slurry and let it simmer until thick, around 1 minute. Add more slurry if the sauce is too thin. Done!

Macros:
