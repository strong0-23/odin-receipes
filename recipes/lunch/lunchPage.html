CHILLI CHEESE FRIES& BEEF

Ingredients:

  • 180g ground beef 95/5
  • 25g pickled Jalapneos
  • 250g potatoes
  • 150g 1% fat cottage cheese
  • 1 slice american cheese
  • 20g skimmed milk
  • 1 medium onion or 50g
  • 2g oil
  • 2g garlic powder
  • 1g cumin
  • 1g chili powder
  • Salt and black pepper
Instructions:
  1. Start with making the fries. Crispy air fryer potatoes.
  2. While the potatoes are baking, add into a blender cottage cheese, american cheese, milk, a pinch of salt, black pepper, and garlic powder into a blender and mix for 20 seconds until smooth.
  3. 10 minutes before the potatoes are ready, heat a pan (24cm/9,5inch) on medium heat, add in oil and add onions. Fry the onions for 2-5 min until caramelized.
  4. Switch to medium-high, add in the beef and keep frying for 1-2 minutes with occasional stirring until the beef is browned. Add Jalapenos, cumin and chilli powder, and keep stirring for another 30 seconds.
  5. Heat up the cheese sauce in the microwave for 20 seconds just until it is warm.
  6. Add fries to a plate, top with beef and then cheese sauce. Enjoy!

Macros:

  • Calories: 655
  • Carbs: 58
  • Protein: 68
  • Fats: 17
