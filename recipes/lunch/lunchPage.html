CHILLI CHEESE FRIES& BEEF

Ingredients:

    180 g ground beef 95/5
    25 g pickled Jalapneos
    250 g potatoes
    150 g 1 % fat cottage cheese
    1 slice american cheese
    20 g skimmed milk
    1 medium onion or 50 g
    2 g oil
    2 g garlic powder
    1 g cumin
    1 g chili powder
    Salt and black pepper
Instructions:
  • Start with making the fries. Crispy air fryer potatoes.
  • While the potatoes are baking, add into a blender cottage cheese, american cheese, milk, a pinch of salt, black pepper, and garlic powder into a blender and mix for 20 seconds until smooth.
  • 10 minutes before the potatoes are ready, heat a pan (24cm/9,5inch) on medium heat, add in oil and add onions. Fry the onions for 2-5 min until caramelized.
  • Switch to medium-high, add in the beef and keep frying for 1-2 minutes with occasional stirring until the beef is browned. Add Jalapenos, cumin and chilli powder, and keep stirring for another 30 seconds.
  • Heat up the cheese sauce in the microwave for 20 seconds just until it is warm.
  • Add fries to a plate, top with beef and then cheese sauce. Enjoy!
